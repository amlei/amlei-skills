# Source and safety rules

## Fact rule

A story may enter production only when every load-bearing fact has at least one credible source.
Reports saying “网传” are not enough. Do not turn an unverified claim into narration.

## Preferred sources

- Mainstream news outlets;
- verified official statements;
- court filings;
- verified celebrity/accounts;
- platform posts by the involved person;
- established entertainment outlets.

## Material rule

- Record the page, outlet, date and URL in `素材清单.md`.
- Use `yt-dlp` only for material you have permission to edit for this workflow.
- Prefer clips where the subject is clearly visible.
- For relationship stories, find same-frame footage.
- Do not use a source interval twice.
- Do not use children’s faces.

## Narrative safety

- Separate verified facts from opinion.
- Do not state guilt beyond the sourced report.
- Keep speculation in the final question, not the narration facts.
- Do not name minors.
- Blur or avoid children entirely.
